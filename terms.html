Terms of Service

Last updated: 25 October 2026

These terms cover Oryx AI pilot engagements: auditable research reports and draft audits offered by the Oryx Centre for Strategic Futures ("Oryx", "we"). Oryx is an early-stage, not-yet-formally-registered research initiative based in Delhi/Noida, India; pilot engagements are currently contracted as individual consultancy. These terms will be updated when Oryx is formally registered. The terms agreed in writing for a specific engagement apply to that engagement.

How it works

We agree the research question and the acceptance criteria in writing before work starts. The report is then built through six stages — question framing, evidence acquisition, claim-level traceability, adversarial review, human decision gates, and monitoring and correction — and no report is delivered without human approval.

Acceptance and payment

The fee is payable only if the report passes the criteria agreed upfront. Acceptance criteria include the sourced-claim rate, every material claim cited, gaps and confidence stated, and human review completed. You have 5 working days from delivery to accept or to state, with reference to the agreed criteria, why the report does not pass. If it does not pass, no fee is due. If it passes, the fee is payable within 7 days of acceptance. One revision round is included where the report falls short of the agreed criteria. Pricing is agreed per pilot or per audit, in writing, before work starts.

Confidentiality

Your question, your draft, and your report are confidential by default. They are never published, never reused, and never shared outside the review of that engagement, with or without attribution, unless you give written permission.

Limits

Reports are a research aid, not legal, medical, or investment advice, and are not a substitute for professional judgement. Our liability for any engagement is capped at the fee paid for that engagement. Nothing in these terms limits liability that cannot be limited by law.

Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of India.
