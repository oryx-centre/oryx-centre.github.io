About

The Oryx Centre for Strategic Futures is a non-profit research and policy initiative working on artificial intelligence, geopolitics, emerging technologies and strategic foresight. We study how AI systems are reshaping research, policy and statecraft, and we build methods for using them accountably — with a particular focus on the perspectives and priorities of the Global South.

Oryx is an early-stage initiative, founded in Delhi, India. We are building our first research programmes and partnerships with research institutions, funders and public bodies internationally.

Research focus

  • Governance and evaluation of agentic AI systems
  • AI, geopolitics and technology competition
  • Emerging technologies and strategic foresight
  • AI policy for the Global South

Working paper

From Answers to Auditable Intelligence: A Governance-First Framework for Agentic AI in Strategic Research and Public Policy
Oryx Centre for Strategic Futures · Working Paper · October 2026

The paper proposes a six-layer framework for making AI-assisted research verifiable rather than merely plausible: question framing; evidence acquisition; claim-level traceability; adversarial review; human decision gates; and post-hoc monitoring. It defines measurable dimensions for agentic research systems — sourced-claim rate, citation precision and recall, evidence coverage, fabrication rate, calibration and selective-prediction behaviour, and human-override outcomes — and sets out an evaluation protocol for testing them.

Our approach

We believe AI systems used in research and policy should be auditable by design: every material claim traceable to its evidence, uncertainty measured rather than hidden, and consequential judgements reserved for accountable humans. Our work is methodological and empirical — frameworks, evaluations and open publications that other institutions can test, reuse and challenge.
