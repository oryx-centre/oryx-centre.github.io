Research — 01

From question to consequence.

The Centre studies how AI systems are reshaping research, policy and statecraft — and builds methods for using them accountably, with a particular focus on the perspectives and priorities of the Global South.

/ 01

Governance & evaluation of agentic AI

How agentic systems should be governed, and how their research outputs can be measured — sourced-claim rate, citation precision and recall, fabrication rate, calibration.

/ 02

AI, geopolitics & technology competition

Strategic research on how advanced AI is redistributing capability and influence between states, institutions and regions.

/ 03

Emerging tech & strategic foresight

Foresight and policy research for the Global South on the technologies — and the rules — that will shape the next decade.

Framework — 02

Built to think in systems.

Our founding framework makes AI-assisted research verifiable rather than merely plausible: a six-layer process in which evidence is acquired, claims are traced, and humans keep the decisions.

Oryx Core Six-layer architecture · V1.0
01

Question framing

The research question is scoped and made answerable before any system runs.

02

Evidence acquisition

Sources are gathered deliberately, with provenance recorded from the start.

03

Claim-level traceability

Every material claim is tied to the specific evidence that supports it.

04

Adversarial review

Outputs are challenged — counter-evidence and weak sourcing are hunted down.

05

Human decision gates

Consequential judgements pause for an accountable human to decide.

06

Monitoring against drift

Systems are re-checked over time as models, sources and contexts change.

Working Paper — 03

The founding paper.

Oryx Centre for Strategic Futures · Working Paper · October 2026
From Answers to Auditable Intelligence: A Governance-First Framework for Agentic AI in Strategic Research and Public Policy

The paper proposes a six-layer framework for making AI-assisted research verifiable rather than merely plausible, and defines measurable dimensions for agentic research systems — with an evaluation protocol for testing them. It is a conceptual framework with protocol targets, published openly for other institutions to test, reuse and challenge.

Philosophy — 04

Auditable by design.
Accountable by default.

We believe AI systems used in research and policy should be auditable by design: every material claim traceable to its evidence, uncertainty measured rather than hidden, and consequential judgements reserved for accountable humans. Our work is methodological and empirical — frameworks, evaluations and open publications.
