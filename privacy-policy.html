Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

This policy describes how the Oryx Centre for Strategic Futures ("Oryx", "we") handles information on this website. Oryx is an early-stage, not-yet-formally-registered research initiative based in Delhi/Noida, India. This policy will be updated when Oryx is formally registered.

What this website collects

Nothing on its own. This website runs no analytics of its own, sets no tracking cookies, and stores nothing you type into it. The guided demo on the Oryx AI page is scripted: it sends nothing to any server and stores nothing. The enquiry forms on this site do not submit to us — they open a pre-filled message in your own email app, and nothing is sent unless you choose to send it.

Email you send us

If you email us (for example at contact@oryxcentre.dpdns.org, or in reply to a pilot offer), we use your message only to reply and, where relevant, to carry out the work you asked for. We do not sell, rent, or share your email address or message content with third parties, and we do not add you to any mailing list unless you explicitly ask to be added.

Drafts sent for audit

If you send us a draft for audit under the "Verify my draft" offer, your draft stays yours. It is never published, never reused, and never shared with anyone outside the review of that audit, with or without attribution.

Outreach email

Our pilot-offer emails are sent to publicly listed business addresses published by the recipient organisations themselves (for example on their official websites and directories). Every message states what is offered and how to reply. If you receive one and do not wish to hear from us again, reply asking to be removed and we will remove that address from our lists and not contact it again.

Links to other sites

This site links to third-party pages (for example Zenodo records and publishers' sites). Their privacy practices are their own; this policy does not cover them.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or requests to be removed from our outreach lists: contact@oryxcentre.dpdns.org.
