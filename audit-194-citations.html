State of citations in AI research briefs

194 citations checked across 30 AI research briefs. Half of them broken. This page stands until a new audit lands — then it is updated, and the old numbers stay visible.

In short: 194 citations, 30 AI research briefs, five verdicts. Only 50 (26%) verified. 95 (49%) broken. This is a pilot audit of AI-generated research briefs — not a universal rate, and not a client result.

The numbers

New audit published 17 November 2026: 10 claims from the ARINTECHNO VERIFY-7 fact-checking guide, checked claim by claim — 9 verified, 1 unverifiable. Read the VERIFY-7 audit ↗

Total: 194 citations across 30 briefs. The denominator is the whole set that was checked — not the 50 that passed.

How it was done

Each citation was resolved, the source was opened, and the source was read against the claim it was attached to — one verdict per citation, recorded in an evidence table. The full method is on the method page, and the step-by-step account is in How we checked the 194 citations.
