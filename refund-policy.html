Refund Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Oryx AI pilot engagements work on pay-only-if-it-passes: the fee is payable only after you accept a report against criteria agreed upfront. This policy follows from that model.

No payment before acceptance

No payment is taken before you accept a report or audit. If you did not accept it, you owe nothing, and there is nothing to refund.

If a paid report missed its agreed criteria

If you paid after acceptance and believe the report did not meet the criteria agreed for that engagement, write to us within 5 working days of payment, quoting the report reference and the criterion you believe was not met. We will review the report against the agreed criteria; where the criteria were not met, the fee for that report is refunded. The same rule applies to draft audits.

How refunds are made

Refunds go to the original payment method. Timing depends on the payment provider once the refund is issued.

Contact

Refund requests and questions: contact@oryxcentre.dpdns.org.
