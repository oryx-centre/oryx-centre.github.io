01

Questions — 05

Asked before you ask.

What is Oryx AI?

An auditable research service from the Oryx team. You ask a research question; the report is built through six stages — question framing, evidence acquisition, claim-level traceability, adversarial review, human decision gates, and monitoring and correction — with every material claim cited and every gap stated.

What does “pay only if it passes” mean?

The pilot fee is payable only if the report passes criteria agreed upfront — including the sourced-claim rate, every material claim cited, gaps and confidence stated, and human review completed. If it does not pass, you pay nothing. Pricing is agreed per pilot.

What makes a report auditable?

Verification numbers are computed mechanically from the report itself, never written by the model that wrote it. Claims without a citation are shown in a separate unsourced section, not buried. An adversarial pass flags problems, and a human reviewer approves the report before delivery.

What happens to my draft or question?

Your draft stays yours. It is never published, never reused, and never shared outside the review of that audit. Questions and reports are confidential by default.

Is Oryx AI a chatbot that answers instantly?

No. There is no instant, ungated answer: every report passes human review before delivery. The guided demo on this page is scripted and is not a live agent.
