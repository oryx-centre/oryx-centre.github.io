Commission a single research report against criteria agreed with you upfront — sourced-claim rate, citation integrity, gaps and confidence stated. If it does not pass, you pay nothing. Pricing is agreed per pilot.

How it works — 01

Six stages, one audit trail.

Built on the Oryx governance framework for agentic AI. The same discipline we apply to our own working papers, applied to your question.

01

Question framing

Your question is sharpened into an answerable brief before any research begins.

02

Evidence acquisition

Real, published sources are gathered — never invented, never cited from memory.

03

Synthesis

A draft report is written from the gathered evidence, with citation markers on its claims.

04

Claim-level verification

Every citation is mechanically checked: does it resolve, and does it say what the claim says it says? Invalid markers are rejected.

05

Adversarial review

Unsupported causations, unsourced material claims and overstatements are flagged — never silently fixed.

06

Human review gate

No report becomes final until a human reviewer approves it. Until then it stays a draft.

What you receive
A report you can defend in the room.

A cited report with a mechanical verification record: how many citations resolved, how many claims carry citations, and which claims could not be sourced — quarantined in plain sight rather than deleted quietly. You then have five working days to accept it against the agreed criteria, or decline it at no cost.

Accountability — 02

We hold the gate on ourselves, too.

Our launch run, on AI adoption in Indian hotels: 15 citations, none invalid, all three sources cited — and a sourced-claim rate of 42%. That report stayed a draft pending human review, exactly as the gate requires. When a draft is weak, it stays a draft. Read the condensed sample report ↗

01

Cited, or quarantined

Claims without a citation are shown in a separate unsourced section, not buried or dressed up.

02

Mechanical, not vibes

Verification numbers are computed from the report itself — never written by the model writing the report.

03

Human, always

The adversarial pass is a flagging pass. The human reviewer is the safeguard that actually counts.
